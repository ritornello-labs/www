Practise the relationship between Traditional and Simplified Chinese character forms.

**611 notes · 611 cards.** This edition has one template: the question shows the Traditional form; reveal its Simplified counterpart, pinyin, English gloss and audio where available. It practises recognizing the Traditional form and connecting it with the Simplified form you know.
