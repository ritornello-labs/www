Learn Japan's 47 prefectures and seven additional regional groupings through map recognition, prefectural flags, location recall, names, and connections.

**54 notes · 263 cards.** Five templates cover highlighted map → place; prefectural flag → Japanese name; named place → location; Chinese place-name reading in Mandarin pinyin; and neighbouring places or waters. Japanese kanji and kana appear on the geography answers. The pinyin drill practises Mandarin, rather than Japanese pronunciation.

## Edition and credit

This is an improved edition of [VectorMaps: Regions and prefectures of Japan](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/2080271721). It retains the original 54-note scope and existing note/card identities while expanding recall directions, presentation and reference material.

This refresh corrects Niigata's spelling and aligns prefecture connections with Japan's official [List of Neighbouring Prefectures, Table 1](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/en/stat-search/file-download?fileKind=2&statInfId=000007646820). That table includes selected connections across water. Named seas/oceans and the seven regional notes retain the source deck's conventions. Prompt labels cover both prefectures and regions, and explicitly identify the Mandarin drill.
