Learn all 22 of Taiwan's [top-level administrative divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Administrative_divisions_of_Taiwan).

**22 notes · 66 cards.** Three complementary families ask you to identify a highlighted division, point to a named division on a blank map, and recall neighbouring divisions or waters. Answers add Chinese names, pinyin, division type, and Wikipedia references.
