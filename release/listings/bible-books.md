## Scope and edition

This edition covers the 73 books of the Catholic canon, including its deuterocanonical books, and gives Protestant sequence alternatives. It does not include the additional books found in Orthodox canons. Tanakh divisions are included for the corresponding books. Authorship answers record traditional attributions rather than settled historical authorship.

Wikipedia references on the book cards provide further context. These are reference and canon drills, not a verse-memorization deck.
